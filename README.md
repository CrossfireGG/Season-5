## Roster & Core Policy

CrossfireGG does not enforce a full roster lock during the regular season or group stage. Teams may adjust players prior to playoffs, subject to tournament administration approval.

All teams must maintain a consistent Team Core (3 players) for competitive integrity.

### VRS Teams

- Must maintain at least three (3) players from the official VRS snapshot roster (May 2, 2026) at all times
- Changing one or two players does not affect VRS eligibility
- Falling below three snapshot players results in immediate disqualification to preserve VRS integrity

### Non-VRS Teams

- Must also maintain a consistent Core-3 identity for competitive integrity
- Administrative review may occur if the core identity changes

### Duplicate Core Restrictions

- **Duplicate core rosters are not allowed** — no two teams may share 3 or more core players
- Teams found in violation will be disqualified
- This rule ensures roster integrity across the competitive landscape

### Coach Substitution Rule (NEW for Season 5)

- Registered team coaches are permitted to substitute into matches as players, provided they are listed on the official roster prior to match start and meet all eligibility requirements.

## Tournament Format

### Group Stage

- **Format:** Round-Robin Group Stage
- **Structure:** 8 Groups × 4 Teams (32 teams total)
- **Match Type:** All matches are Best-of-3 (BO3)
- **Duration:** June 1 – June 18, 2026
- **Advancement:** Top 2 teams from each group advance to Playoffs (16 teams total)
- **Default Match Day:** Day 3 of each round window @ 8:00 PM EST
- Teams may reschedule matches within the group-stage window with mutual agreement

**Match Rounds:**

- Round 1: June 1 – June 4
- Round 2: June 5 – June 8
- Round 3: June 9 – June 12
- **Buffer / Make-up Matches:** June 13 – June 18 *(NEW for Season 5)*

**Break Week:** June 19 – June 22 *(shorter than Season 4)*

### Seeding Method

Teams are seeded using a **separated seeding methodology** to ensure competitive balance:

1. **Seeds 1-8** are placed in separate groups — one top seed per group
2. **Seeds 9-16** are distributed across all groups
3. **Seeds 17-24** are distributed across all groups
4. **Seeds 25-32** are distributed across all groups

This ensures each group contains a mix of skill levels and prevents stacking.

### Playoff Stage

- **Format:** 16-Team Single Elimination Bracket
- **Match Type:** All matches are Best-of-3 (BO3)
- **Duration:** June 23 – July 10, 2026
- **Default Match Day:** Day 3 of each round window @ 8:00 PM EST
- Reschedules permitted within each round window with admin approval

**Playoff Rounds:**

- Round 4 (Round of 16): June 23 – June 26
- Round 5 (Quarterfinals): June 27 – June 30
- Round 6 (Semi-Finals): July 1 – July 5
- Round 7 (Grand Finals): July 6 – July 10

## Match Integrity & Administration

### Competitive Platform

- **Platform:** All matches hosted on FACEIT
- **Server Region:** North America (United States)
- **Server Settings:** Official competitive CS2 settings (MR12/3)

### Anti-Cheat Enforcement

- **Required:** FACEIT Anti-Cheat (AC) is mandatory for all matches
- Matches will not start if any player does not have AC enabled
- Players must run FACEIT AC during all matches — failure results in **match forfeit**
- Players with **active VAC bans or platform bans are ineligible**

### Camera Requirement (VRS Integrity Rule — NEW for Season 5)

- Players must have a working camera setup available upon request
- Admins may request players to turn on cameras at any time during matches for integrity verification, especially in VRS-eligible matches

### Forfeit Rule (Updated for Season 5)

- Any match not played within the scheduled round window without approved rescheduling will be recorded as a **forfeit loss**

### Head-to-Head Tiebreaker (NEW for Season 5)

- In the event of a tie in group standings, the head-to-head result between tied teams will be used as the **primary tiebreaker** before round differential

### Demo Recording

- All official matches are recorded and archived
- Demos available for admin review and dispute resolution
- Demo access provided through FACEIT platform
